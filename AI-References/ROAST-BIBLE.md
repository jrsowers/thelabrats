## Who may be roasted

`SOUL.md` has authority here, and it has two tiers.

**League managers.** Twelve real people, most of them friends, none of whom
signed up to be public figures. Roast their decisions, never their lives. No
job, no family, no appearance, no finances, nothing outside fantasy football.

**NFL players.** Public figures being paid publicly to make choices the whole
sport discusses. Their decisions, on and off the field, are fair game. Aim at
the choice, or at the institution that tolerated it. **Never at the people
harmed by it.** Say *alleged* where it is alleged.

### The league is not twelve guys

**Four of the twelve managers are women.** A headline reading "Twelve Men Walk
Into A Draft Room" shipped before anyone caught it.

Never a gendered collective: no "twelve men", "the guys", "gentlemen", "boys",
"fellas". Use **managers**, **the room**, **the league**, **everybody**, **all
twelve**. Individual pronouns are already correct from names — it is only the
collective nouns that slip. "Guy" about one NFL player is fine.

## What this league actually is

Facts the jokes have to be built on:

- **Superflex.** The OP slot takes any offensive player and in practice takes a
  second quarterback. **Twenty-four quarterbacks start every week.** One QB on a
  roster is not a preference, it is a hole in the lineup card.
- **Only five bench spots.** Fifteen roster slots, fifteen rounds. Every
  duplicate is a spot that cannot hold a bye-week fill-in.
- **Half PPR, six-point passing touchdowns.** Quarterbacks are worth more here
  than on any list anyone read before the draft.
- **One TE slot, one FLEX, one K, one D/ST.** A third tight end has nowhere to
  go on a Sunday.
- **Roster caps:** 4 QB, 8 RB, 8 WR, 3 TE, 2 K, 2 D/ST.

## What the joke is about

`ROAST-WRITER.md` says never to build a punchline whose only content is a rank
difference. This is the local version of that rule, in priority order.

**1. The player.** You are given his analyst blurb as `playerStory`. Read it
first. Old, unproven, hurt, in trouble, talked about for years and never
actually done it — the joke is usually sitting in there.

**2. The roster.** You are given `rosterProblems` in plain language. One
quarterback in a format that starts two. Three tight ends for one slot. A kicker
eight rounds early.

**3. The numbers.** Last, and rarely. At most one, and only when the number is
absurd on its face — 790 places for a kicker is funny, twenty-two better players
is not. **Never open on one.**

## Accuracy

Every fact comes from the fact sheet or the news dossier. Nothing else exists.

**Nothing may be extrapolated forward.** "Suffered a groin injury on 8 August"
does not license "he is still questionable" — that is a new claim about today,
invented. Report what happened. Say nothing about what is true now unless a
note states it. This has been broken before and is now rejected by a check.

American spelling. This is an American league watching American football, and
British spellings leaked in once because this document was written with them.

## Headings need a payoff

For recap section headings and report-card verdicts only.

> ❌ Mike Built A Quarterback Room
> ✅ Mike Built A Quarterback Room, And Not Much Else

The first half sets up, the second turns. **A heading with no turn is a label,
not a joke.**
